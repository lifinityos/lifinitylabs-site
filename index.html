The problem

Many applicants are filtered before a human reads their resume. ATS systems can reject qualified candidates due to formatting that doesn’t parse correctly, missing or misaligned keywords, and unclear role positioning.

✓

Career teams can coach — but rewriting and keyword-aligning resumes at scale is time-intensive. This service reduces staff load while improving ATS compatibility and recruiter clarity.

Our solution

ATS-compatible structure

Single-column, parser-friendly formatting designed to improve ATS readability while remaining recruiter-friendly.

Role-aligned keyword alignment

Alignment to a target job title and job description to improve relevance signals for ATS screening.

Recruiter clarity & impact

Edits to improve clarity, structure, and phrasing so candidates are evaluated accurately by human reviewers.

Human-reviewed quality control

Technology-enabled drafting and checks with human review for consistency, professionalism, and policy alignment.

How a pilot works

  1. Confirm scope: volume, turnaround SLA, and delivery method.
  2. Intake: students submit through a secure institutional intake link, or your team submits centrally.
  3. Optimization: resumes are optimized for ATS compatibility and recruiter clarity.
  4. Delivery: files returned by email to the student or program coordinator (your choice).
  5. Review: basic usage and SLA reporting available (aggregate, non-sensitive).

Typical pilot turnaround is 24–48 hours depending on volume. Formal SLAs are set during pilot setup.

FAQ

Do we need to deploy software or integrate systems?

No. This is a service-based offering. We provide a secure intake workflow and deliver completed files via your chosen delivery model.

How is quality controlled?

We use technology-enabled drafting and checks with human review for consistency, professionalism, and ATS-safe structure.

Can you support cohorts or high-volume periods?

Yes. Volume and turnaround SLAs are defined during pilot setup. We can structure pilots around cohorts, semesters, or rolling intake.

How do institutional payments work?

Institutional engagements are typically invoiced (Net terms), with structure based on pilot/semester/annual or volume agreements.

What do you need from students or participants?

At minimum: the current resume and a target job title plus job description text or link. Optional notes can be included for context.
